# Which Stainless Strength Number Should You Trust?

The biggest number on a datasheet is usually tensile strength. It is not usually the number that sizes the part. For brackets, pressure parts, supports, and load-bearing sheet, yield strength is the first number to read.

Stainless strength depends on family and condition. Annealed [304](/grades/304-stainless-steel) behaves like a formable general-purpose stainless. [2205 duplex](/grades/2205-duplex-stainless-steel) can more than double its yield strength. 17-4PH can go much higher again, but it is a different family with different corrosion limits.

## Read the Certificate in This Order

| MTC line | What it tells you | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Yield strength Rp0.2 | Where permanent deformation starts | Main design number for most loaded parts |
| Tensile strength Rm | Maximum stress before fracture | Useful limit, but not the working design basis |
| Elongation | How much ductility remains | Decides bending, drawing, and cracking risk |
| Condition or temper | Annealed, cold-worked, aged, hard | Same grade name can behave very differently |

If a part is going to bend, draw, or form, elongation matters as much as strength. A very strong strip with low elongation can crack where annealed material would form cleanly.

## How Common Grades Compare

| Grade / condition | Yield strength, typical minimum | Where it fits |
|---|---:|---|
| 316L annealed | 170 MPa | Corrosion-first welded service |
| 304 annealed | 205 MPa | General fabrication and forming |
| 430 annealed | 205 MPa | Indoor ferritic work, lower ductility |
| 201 annealed | 260 MPa | Indoor cost-sensitive work, higher work hardening |
| 2205 duplex | 450 MPa | Strength plus chloride margin |
| 17-4PH H900 | 1,170 MPa | High-strength machined parts, shafts, aerospace-style duty |

The table also shows why "stainless is weaker than carbon steel" is too broad. Annealed 304 has lower yield than A36 carbon steel, but duplex and PH stainless can move far beyond it.

## When Cold Work Changes Everything

Austenitic stainless can be strengthened by cold work. Rolling, drawing, and stamping raise strength by increasing dislocation density and, in some grades, forming strain-induced martensite.

That is useful for springs, clips, precision strip, and formed parts that need final stiffness. It is risky if the material still needs deep drawing or sharp bends afterward. Full-hard 304 is strong, but it is no longer a friendly forming grade.

Always check condition words such as annealed, 1/4 hard, 1/2 hard, full hard, solution annealed, or aged. A grade name without condition is only half the mechanical specification.

## When Duplex Saves Weight

Duplex enters the design when 304 or 316L requires too much wall thickness or too much section size. Because 2205 yield strength is roughly twice that of 304, pressure or structural parts can sometimes be made thinner while gaining chloride resistance.

That does not make duplex a universal upgrade. It is less forgiving in welding and less suitable for deep drawing. Use it when strength, chloride, or weight actually drives the job, not because a higher yield number looks better on a chart.

## When PH Grades Belong

Precipitation-hardening grades such as 17-4PH are built for high-strength machined components: shafts, fasteners, valve parts, aerospace fittings, and precision hardware. Their strength comes from aging treatment, not from ordinary annealed austenitic behavior.

They are not replacements for 304 or 316L sheet in ordinary corrosion service. The corrosion envelope, heat treatment route, machining allowance, and final hardness all need to be specified.

## A Cleaner Strength Rule

For general sheet and welded fabrication, start with 304, 304L, or 316L and check yield only after the environment is right. For thinner, stronger chloride-service parts, review duplex. For very high strength, move to PH grades and treat the order as a heat-treatment specification, not a simple grade substitution.

Strength is only useful when the steel still survives the environment and can still be fabricated into the part.
