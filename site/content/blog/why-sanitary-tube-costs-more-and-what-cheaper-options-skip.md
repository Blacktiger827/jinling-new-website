# Why Is Sanitary Tube Priced Above Industrial Pipe?

Sanitary tube is not expensive because the words sound cleaner. It costs more because the route is different.

Industrial pipe can be acceptable for pressure, structure, or utility service. Sanitary tube has to survive a hygiene review: weld profile, inner surface, cleanability, testing, traceability, and packing.

## What the Premium Pays For

| Cost layer | What it protects |
|---|---|
| cleaner production route | keeps contamination and handling damage lower |
| controlled weld and anneal route | reduces weld-line defects and corrosion risk |
| inner surface control | keeps residue from hiding inside the tube |
| per-length test discipline | catches leaks or wall defects before shipment |
| heat and bundle traceability | supports food, dairy, pharma, or sanitary QA |
| end protection and clean packing | keeps the tube usable when it arrives |

When a quote is much closer to industrial pipe pricing, ask which of these layers disappeared.

## When Industrial Pipe Is Fine

Industrial pipe is not inferior in its own lane. It can be the right route for structure, utility lines, drainage, pressure service, and non-hygienic process work where the inside surface is not part of a cleanability claim.

The mistake is not buying industrial pipe. The mistake is buying industrial pipe while asking it to pass as sanitary tube.

## Ask What Will Be Measured

Sanitary tube claims need measured evidence. A supplier should be able to say what surface is controlled, how weld quality is checked, what test record exists, and how each bundle remains traceable.

If the answer stays at "it looks polished", the quote is not yet a sanitary quote. It is a visual quote.

## What Cheap Routes Usually Skip

The shortcuts are rarely announced. They show up as vague wording:

- "polished" without inner-surface target
- "sanitary style" instead of a real standard
- no per-length test record
- mixed workshop with industrial tube
- weak end protection
- MTC available only after shipment

Any one of those may be acceptable for a non-hygienic project. They are not acceptable if the tube will touch food, dairy, pharma product, or clean process fluid.

## What to Put on the PO

Write the actual sanitary route:

> "316L sanitary tube, standard and dimension stated, ID/OD finish route stated, weld and test records required, heat traceability maintained, ends protected before export packing."

If the application is not hygienic, do not overbuy sanitary tube. But if it is hygienic, do not ask industrial pipe to do sanitary work quietly.
