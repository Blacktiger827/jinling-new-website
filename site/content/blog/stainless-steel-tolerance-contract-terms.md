# What Tolerance Wording Actually Protects a Stainless Steel PO?

"2.0 mm stainless sheet" is not a tolerance clause.

If the PO does not name the dimensional standard, class, minimum acceptable thickness, flatness expectation, measurement method, and remedy, the order usually falls back to the mill's normal tolerance. That may be fine for general fabrication. It may be a problem for laser cutting, pressure work, tight assemblies, or a buyer expecting no-minus material.

The clause should be boring. Boring is good. Boring gets enforced.

## The Six Things to Write

| Clause item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Product standard | Chemistry/mechanical route, such as ASTM A240 or EN 10088. |
| Dimensional standard | Tolerance route, such as ASTM A480, EN, or GB/T. |
| Tolerance class | Normal, precision, special, class A/B/C, as applicable. |
| Minimum thickness | Stops "below nominal but within tolerance" surprises. |
| Flatness / edge / length | Controls fabrication fit beyond thickness alone. |
| Remedy | Defines replacement, credit, rework, or rejection path. |

If one of these matters and is not written, it is not controlled.

## Say "No Minus" Only If You Mean It

No-minus thickness means delivered material cannot fall below nominal. It usually costs more because the supplier must roll or select stock with extra margin.

Write it like this:

> Thickness 2.0 mm, +0.10 / -0.00 mm. Minimum delivered thickness 2.00 mm at any measured point outside excluded edge zones.

Do not write "2.0 mm exact" or "good thickness." Those are future arguments, not requirements.

## A Practical PO Clause

For a common sheet order:

> 304 stainless sheet per ASTM A240. Dimensional tolerance per ASTM A480. Thickness 2.0 mm nominal, minimum delivered thickness 1.95 mm. Width 1219 mm ±0.5 mm. Length 2438 mm +3 / -0 mm. Surface 2B. Measurement by calibrated micrometer, five readings per sampled sheet. Non-conforming lots subject to replacement, credit, or written concession by buyer approval.

For a tighter order:

> 316L stainless sheet per ASTM A240 / EN 10088-2. Thickness 3.0 mm +0.10 / -0.00 mm. Flatness per agreed precision class or maximum 3 mm per 1000 mm on reference surface. PMI and dimensional report required before shipment. Rework or substitution only with written approval.

The second clause costs more. That is normal. Precision is a product feature, not a wish.

## Do Not Separate the Remedy

Many POs specify dimensions well and then forget what happens if the lot fails. Add the remedy before shipment:

- supplier replaces at their cost
- buyer may accept with written concession
- credit amount tied to weight shortfall or rework cost
- third-party measurement governs if readings are disputed
- no substitution without written approval

This keeps the claim from becoming a new negotiation after the material arrives.

## Contract Shortcut

Write tolerance like someone at receiving will have to use it with a micrometer in hand. Name the standard, class, minimum, method, and remedy. If the inspector cannot tell pass/fail from the PO, the clause is not finished.
