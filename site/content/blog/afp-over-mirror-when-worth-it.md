# When Is AFP Over Mirror Worth Paying For?

AFP over mirror looks optional until the building opens. After that, it becomes a maintenance decision.

Bare mirror shows fingerprints quickly because it reflects light cleanly. A finger mark interrupts that reflection, so the panel looks dirty even when the surface is only lightly touched. AFP lowers the surface energy and helps oil marks wipe away with less smearing. The result is not a scratch-proof mirror. It is a mirror that is easier to live with.

That order matters: polish first, inspect the mirror, then decide whether AFP earns its place. AFP should not be used to hide haze, pinholes, poor grade selection, or careless packing.

## Where AFP Over Mirror Makes Sense

Use AFP over mirror when the panel is both visible and touched.

Good routes include:

- elevator door skins and hand-adjacent cab panels
- premium appliance fronts
- hospitality counters and reception panels
- branded retail surfaces that staff wipe constantly

In these places, the owner is not paying for brighter mirror. The owner is paying for fewer visible touch marks between cleaning cycles.

## Where AFP Is Usually a Waste

AFP is usually not the first answer when:

- the panel is out of reach
- the surface is low-touch
- the environment is coastal or chloride-heavy
- the real risk is poor packing, cutting, or site handling
- the mirror itself has haze, pinholes, or gloss inconsistency

If the alloy is wrong, choose the right alloy. If the mirror is weak, fix the polishing route. If the problem is scratches, fix packing and handling. AFP comes after those decisions, not before them.

It also needs the right film. A film that works on bare mirror may mark, pull, or leave residue on an AFP surface if the tack and removal window are wrong.

## The Elevator Test

Elevators show the decision clearly.

A mirror ceiling panel may not need AFP because nobody touches it. A mirror door skin almost always needs a touch review because people brush against it, cleaners wipe it, and the panel is judged every day under strong light.

That means the spec should be by touch zone:

| Panel location | AFP decision |
|---|---|
| ceiling feature | optional |
| rear wall feature | depends on touch and traffic |
| door skin | usually worth review |
| button-adjacent panel | usually worth it |
| coastal elevator | solve alloy first, AFP second |

## What to Write on the PO

Do not write `mirror with AFP` and stop.

Write the route:

> "304 No.8 mirror, AFP top route after approved mirror sample, AFP-compatible protective film, installation handling agreed."

For coastal or chloride-cleaned interiors:

> "316L No.8 mirror, AFP by touch-zone review, cleaning chemistry confirmed before handover."

Also confirm color or visual matching if panels will be ordered in phases. AFP lots should be compared back to a retained reference sample.

## The Clean Decision

AFP over mirror is worth paying for when the daily complaint would otherwise be "this expensive panel always looks marked." It is not worth paying for when the panel is already protected by location, or when the real failure mode is grade, polish, or packaging.
