## Packaging Solutions

Our stainless steel coils and slit coils are packed with export-standard seaworthy packaging to ensure safe transportation and efficient handling. We offer multiple packing options including Eye to Sky packing (vertical coil packing), Eye to Wall packing (horizontal coil packing), and single slit coil packing to meet different loading, storage, and processing requirements.

Each stainless steel coil can be protected with PE / PVC film, VCI paper, edge protectors, steel strapping, and wooden or steel pallets, providing reliable protection during sea export, warehouse storage, and container transportation.
