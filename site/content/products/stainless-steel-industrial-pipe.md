# Industrial Stainless Steel Pipe for ASTM A312 / A790 Pressure Service

**Choose industrial pipe when schedule, wall, and proof control the order.** If the job is mainly about visible finish or hygienic bore, move to the other tube families.

Jinling supplies industrial pipe from NPS 1/2 to 24 in 304L, 316L, 2205, and 2507 across welded and seamless supply. The useful buying split is normally pressure category first: austenitic A312 welded, austenitic A312 seamless, or duplex A790.

Pressure orders usually go wrong when buyers ask for size first and leave the code basis open. Fix the standard and proof package before debating price.

## Write the Pressure Route First

Industrial pipe belongs where schedule, wall, pressure proof, and code-facing documents control the order. If the real issue is hygienic bore or visible finish, move to sanitary tube or decorative pipe before pricing.

**General pressure service** usually begins with ASTM A312 language: schedule, wall tolerance, hydro test, and MTC evidence.

**Seamless-mandated specifications** need OD, wall, size traceability, and delivery condition agreed before the quote is treated as comparable.

**Chloride, sour, or seawater duty** may move into ASTM A790 duplex review, with PMI, PREN, hardness, and tighter release proof named together.

## Which Pressure Category Fits the Spec?

| Pressure category | Standard | When it fits | Release proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Austenitic welded pipe | *ASTM A312* / *A358* | Most process lines, larger OD, standard commercial supply | Hydro + eddy current + wall check |
| Austenitic seamless pipe | *ASTM A312* / *A376* | Smaller OD, seamless-mandated specs, higher fatigue sensitivity | Hydro + wall check + size traceability |
| Duplex pipe | *ASTM A790* | Chloride-heavy, higher-strength, sour-service or seawater duty | Hydro + eddy + PMI + duplex chemistry / hardness |

## Where Pressure-Pipe Proof Matters

**Wall tolerance is tighter than the code minimum.** Jinling holds ≤ 10% negative wall tolerance where *ASTM A312* allows 12.5%.

**Proof is 100%, not sample-based.** Hydro test, eddy current, and wall verification stay attached to the release logic instead of becoming optional extras.

**Duplex paperwork is ready for real projects.** 2205 and 2507 orders can ship with PMI, PREN, hardness, and sour-service support where the job needs it.

304L is the common choice for general process service. 316L is the normal chloride upgrade. 2205 and 2507 belong where strength, chloride exposure, or sour-service proof justify tighter release. Use the [grade library](/grades) when the line still needs a chemistry decision.

## Order Notes Before Release

**Standard and schedule.** Put *A312*, *A790*, or the exact project code basis on the PO together with NPS and schedule.

**Seamless versus welded.** If the job truly needs seamless, say so early. Above certain sizes, welded is the normal commercial path.

**Proof package.** Confirm hydro, eddy current, PMI, hardness, NACE, end prep, and MTC format before production.

## FAQ

**Is 100% hydrostatic testing standard?** Yes. Every pipe is hydro-tested at 24 bar for at least 1 minute, with records tied to the shipment file.

**Why does the wall tolerance matter so much?** Because the pressure calculation lives on minimum wall, not nominal wall. A tighter negative tolerance reduces uncertainty at inspection and handover.

**Can Jinling supply NACE-ready 2205 pipe?** Yes. Duplex orders can be released with hardness and chemistry support for sour-service projects when it is specified at order stage.

**What is the normal size limit for seamless pipe?** Seamless is common up to about OD 219 mm. Above that, welded becomes the normal commercial supply.

**Can schedule and grade be mixed in one order?** Yes. Mixed NPS, schedule, and grade loading is routine as long as each line keeps its own documentation.
