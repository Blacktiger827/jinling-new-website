# China Stainless Steel Market Note: Oct 13 to Oct 29, 2025

Late October was pushed around by macro and tariff headlines more than by clean physical demand. Stainless futures recovered part of the period, but buyers still had to separate policy noise from real stainless fundamentals.

For buyers, this was a week to be calm. A headline can move sentiment. It does not automatically move the container price unless mills, inventory, and downstream orders move with it.

## What Mattered

| Signal | Buyer read |
|---|---|
| tariff noise stayed high | origin and landed-cost checks mattered |
| 304 futures recovered part of the period | sentiment improved, but needed physical support |
| cost support remained relevant | sellers had some floor under offers |
| freight recovered on some routes | shipment timing affected landed cost |

This was not a market for one-line answers. A supplier saying "price is going up" needed to explain whether the reason was futures, mill list price, raw material cost, or actual spot shortage.

## Buyer Use

For buyers with fixed project deadlines, late October was a planning week. Confirm material route first, then decide whether market timing justified immediate release.

For buyers comparing China with alternative origins, this was also a week to check whether the origin saving survived after freight, lead time, documentation, and available specification range were included.

## Buyer Takeaway This Period

Do not buy the headline. Buy the route. If price, origin, documents, and delivery timing all make sense, the order can move. If only the headline is urgent, wait for the physical market to confirm it.
