# No.4 Brushed Finish Datasheet

No.4 is the practical satin route. It is less formal than hairline and easier to live with on real equipment.

Jinling check: use this when a satin face matters more than reflection. Verify grit, grain consistency, film, and whether AFP is needed in touch zones.

## Use This When

Use No.4 for kitchen equipment fronts, appliance panels, and general visible faces when practical satin is enough.

Lock the visible side and grain direction before cutting. If touch marks are part of daily use, consider AFP after the base finish is approved. If the brief wants a longer, finer line, compare hairline first instead of forcing No.4 to do both jobs.

## Release Notes

- Roughness: Ra 0.2-0.4 um is typical.
- Abrasive setup: 150-180 grit equivalent.
- Visual effect: satin directional grain.
- Main value: practical appearance and repairability.
- Protection: film and grain direction should be named before release.

## Verify Before PO

1. Confirm finish, grain expectation, and visible face.
2. Confirm substrate and whether service is indoor, wet-cleaned, or exposed.
3. Confirm film and handling route.
4. If the job needs a finer, more linear look, compare against hairline instead of forcing No.4 to do both jobs.
