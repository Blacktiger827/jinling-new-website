# BA Finish Datasheet

BA is a bright-annealed mill finish. It is cleaner and brighter than 2B, but it is not mirror polish.

Jinling check: use this when the buyer needs a bright mill face or polishing base. Decide early whether BA stays final or moves into AFP or mirror processing.

## Use This When

Use BA for bright indoor panels, appliance faces, and polishing base work when mill brightness is enough.

Confirm that BA is acceptable as a mill finish, not polish. For touch-heavy visible faces, consider AFP after the BA sample is approved. If reflection itself is the brief, move to 8K mirror instead of stretching BA into a promise it cannot honestly carry.

## Release Notes

- Roughness: Ra 0.05-0.10 um is typical.
- Visual effect: smooth reflective mill finish, not mirror polish.
- Directionality: no obvious directional grain.
- Main use: bright visible face or polish base.
- Protection: handling marks show easily, so film and packing matter.

## Verify Before PO

1. State whether BA is the final face or a base for AFP or mirror processing.
2. Confirm substrate and finish together.
3. Confirm film and handling route.
4. If the job is indoor bright work, BA is often enough. If it needs mirror, move up.
