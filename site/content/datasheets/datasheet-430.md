# 430 Stainless Steel Datasheet

430 is the magnetic indoor value grade. It is useful when the part stays dry and the visible face matters more than chloride margin.

Jinling check: use this when the job wants nickel-free value, magnetism, or appliance-facing indoor sheet. Verify the dry-use boundary before quote approval.

## Use This When

Use 430 for dry indoor panels, appliances, and trim when nickel-free value or magnetic behavior is part of the brief.

Name the magnetic requirement clearly. If wet cleaning, outdoor air, or coastal humidity enters the job, move to 304 or 316L instead of forcing 430 into a wet-service role.

## Core Data

- Standard IDs: UNS S43000; EN 1.4016.
- Chemistry band: Cr 16.0-18.0%; Ni no more than 0.75%.
- Mechanical baseline: tensile minimum 450 MPa; yield minimum 205 MPa.
- Corrosion cue: PREN is about 17, so dry indoor exposure is the natural lane.

## Stop Before Release

- 430 is magnetic in normal condition.
- It is strong indoor value, not an outdoor corrosion route.
- If the job needs wet cleaning or chloride margin, move up instead of forcing 430.

## Finish and Form Note

2B, BA, No.4, Hairline, mirror, and AFP are common finish routes. Coil, sheet, strip, and foil are standard forms.

## Verify Before PO

1. Confirm `430 / S43000` or the required equivalent.
2. Confirm the service is dry and indoor.
3. Confirm whether magnetism is part of the brief.
4. If the job sounds like a wet or coastal application, switch the grade first.
