# Hairline Finish Datasheet

Hairline is the long directional brushed route. It works best when grain continuity matters more than reflection.

Jinling check: use this when the design needs a calm, continuous line. Verify grain direction, lot continuity, film, and installation handling before release.

## Use This When

Use hairline for elevator walls, long panels, and architectural faces when one continuous grain matters.

Direction mistakes change the whole wall reading. If panels will be cut, bent, or installed in sequence, keep direction, lot continuity, panel order, and film plan together. If repairability matters more than a fine line, compare No.4 before release.

## Release Notes

- Roughness: Ra 0.2-0.4 um is typical, often lower with finer grit.
- Abrasive setup: 180-240 grit equivalent.
- Grain: continuous and directional.
- Visual effect: low to medium reflectivity.
- Protection: film should stay on until the last realistic handling stage.

## Verify Before PO

1. Write the grain direction into the order.
2. Confirm visible-face sample and lot continuity.
3. Confirm film, bend, and installation route.
4. If the job needs a more repairable satin look, compare against No.4 instead of guessing.
