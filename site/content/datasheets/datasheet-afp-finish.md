# AFP Finish Datasheet

AFP is a clear anti-fingerprint stack. It lowers visible prints and wipe marks, but it does not replace the right alloy or base finish.

Jinling check: use this when touch performance and cleaning reality justify the coating. Verify base finish, coating thickness, film compatibility, and approved sample before release.

## Use This When

Use AFP for high-touch indoor panels, appliance fronts, and elevator accents when fingerprints are the daily problem.

Approve the sample when tone, gloss, or coating feel matters. Confirm the cleaner and wipe routine expected after installation. If the real risk is chloride, scratching, or outdoor exposure, solve the grade and service route before adding AFP.

## Release Notes

- Dry film: 10-20 um is typical.
- Hardness: 2H-3H is typical.
- Appearance: clear or sample-matched slight tone.
- Main value: lower print visibility and easier wipe cleaning.
- Protection: film must match laser, bending, storage, and final installation route.

## Verify Before PO

1. Name grade, base finish, AFP, and film together.
2. Confirm the sample if color, gloss, or touch feel matters.
3. Confirm bend, laser, storage, and installation route before production.
4. Do not use AFP to rescue a weak corrosion choice.
