## 2. What's Inside Each Datasheet

Every datasheet follows the same structure so you can compare grades and finish systems side by side:

**Chemical Composition** 鈥?Element-by-element breakdown (C, Cr, Ni, Mo, Mn, Si, P, S, N) with ASTM/EN specification limits and typical mill values from our stock. This is the section your quality team needs to verify grade authenticity against incoming MTCs.

**Mechanical Properties** 鈥?Tensile strength, yield strength (0.2% proof), elongation, and hardness (HV/HRB/HRC) at room temperature. Values listed as both specification minimums and typical achieved values. For 2205 duplex, impact toughness (Charpy V-notch at -46C) is included.

**Physical Properties** 鈥?Density, thermal conductivity, specific heat capacity, coefficient of thermal expansion, electrical resistivity, and elastic modulus. Essential for engineering calculations involving heat transfer, structural deflection, or electrical grounding.

**Corrosion Resistance** 鈥?Pitting Resistance Equivalent Number (PREN) calculation, general corrosion rates in common media (nitric acid, sulfuric acid, sodium chloride), and critical pitting temperature (CPT) where applicable. Includes guidance on which environments each grade handles and where it fails.

**Available Product Forms** 鈥?Coil, sheet, plate, strip, tube/pipe, and bar 鈥?with standard dimension ranges Jinling stocks. Thickness, width, and length ranges for flat products; OD and wall thickness for tubular.

**Surface Finishes** 鈥?Available finishes (2B, BA, No.4, Hairline, 8K Mirror, AFP, Embossed) with surface roughness (Ra) and glossiness (GU) values measured in our facility. Finish datasheets also explain substrate selection, protective film, and visual-acceptance logic.

**Applicable Standards** 鈥?Full cross-reference table: ASTM, EN, JIS, GB (Chinese national standard), and relevant product standards (e.g., ASTM A240 for flat products, ASTM A312 for pipe).

## 3. How to Read a Mill Test Certificate (MTC)

Every shipment from Jinling Steel includes the mill's EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificate. This is a document issued by the producing mill 鈥?not by the trader or distributor 鈥?certifying that the material meets specified requirements based on actual testing of the supplied heat or lot.

**Key values to verify on any MTC:**

- **Heat number** 鈥?Unique identifier for the steel melt. Enables full traceability from raw material to finished product.
- **Chemical composition** 鈥?Actual analysis values, not specification limits alone. Compare Cr, Ni, Mo, and C against the relevant ASTM or EN standard to confirm grade compliance.
- **Mechanical test results** 鈥?Tensile strength, yield strength, elongation, and hardness. All values must meet or exceed the specification minimum.
- **Dimensional inspection** 鈥?Thickness, width, and flatness tolerance confirmation.
- **Surface condition** 鈥?Finish designation and any noted surface defects.

A common procurement error is accepting an MTC that lists only specification ranges without actual tested values. An EN 10204 3.1 certificate must include specific test results from the actual material being shipped.

- **2507 Super Duplex** 鈥?PREN > 40 for severe chloride and high-temperature environments
