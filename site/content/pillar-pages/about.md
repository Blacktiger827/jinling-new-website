# About Jinling Steel

Jinling Steel is a Foshan-based stainless processor and exporter founded in 1997. The company works around a simple problem: many stainless orders look clear on a quote sheet, then become difficult when grade, finish, processing route, packing, documents, and shipment timing are handled as separate decisions.

Our role is to keep those decisions close enough that the order can be reviewed before it becomes expensive to correct.

## What We Actually Control

We do not claim to make every stainless product in the market. The work we lead is narrower and more practical:

- Stainless coil, sheet, bar, tube, pipe, and selected processed forms.
- Surface routes including 2B, BA, No.4, hairline, 8K mirror, AFP, and protective film.
- Slitting, cut-to-length, coating, film application, export packing, and release checks.
- Grade, finish, document, packing, and shipment decisions that need to stay aligned before the PO is released.

This is why the site is organized by product form, surface finish, grade, application, capability, datasheet, and buying problem. A buyer should not have to treat those as disconnected pages.

## What the Factory Proof Means

The useful proof is not a slogan about reliability. It is more ordinary than that: real production photos, QC stations, export packing, document checks, and people who can explain why one route is safer than another.

The numbers matter, but they should stay in context:

- Founded in 1997 in Foshan, Guangdong.
- 25,000 m2 dual-facility footprint.
- 60+ automated production lines across processing and finishing routes.
- 150+ active clients across 10+ countries.
- About 30% of new work comes through repeat clients and referrals.

Those figures do not replace technical review. They simply show that the operating base is real enough to inspect.

## How Orders Hold Together

Most avoidable problems happen in the handoff: one team quotes, another processes, another packs, another explains the document file. We try to reduce that drift.

Incoming material is checked against the mill basis before processing. Surface, dimension, film, and packing decisions are reviewed as part of release work, not as late decoration. Export documents are treated as part of the order, especially when MTC, PMI, COO, inspection timing, or destination rules matter.

The goal is not maximum complexity. It is a cleaner order path.

## Where We Draw the Line

Commercial trust also means knowing what not to overstate.

- If 304 is enough, 316L should not be sold by habit.
- If 201 is outside its honest service window, price should not hide that risk.
- If a tolerance depends on thickness, width, edge route, or downstream process, it should be confirmed before it is promised.
- If a China market note is dated, it should not be treated as a live offer.

This is the tone we want buyers to feel across the site: practical, specific, and careful with claims.
