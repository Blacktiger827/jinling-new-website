# Stainless Steel Grades

*Five core grades held in stock. 904L, 17-4PH, and Inconel available on request.*

Jinling Steel's philosophy: **the just-right grade is the best grade.** We match chemistry and PREN to your service environment — no upselling 316L when 304 performs, no downselecting 201 where chloride demands Mo.

## Core Grades

| Grade | PREN | Best Fit |
|---|:---:|---|
| [304 / 304L](/grades/304-stainless-steel) | ≈ 19 | General-purpose austenitic; food, interior, mild atmospheric |
| [316 / 316L](/grades/316l-stainless-steel) | ≈ 25 | Mo-bearing; coastal, pharma, chloride ≥ 200 ppm |
| [430](/grades/430-stainless-steel) | ≈ 17 | Ferritic; appliance panels, **No.8 Mirror capable** |
| [201](/grades/201-stainless-steel) | ≈ 16 | Cr-Mn-N; budget indoor decorative |
| [2205 Duplex](/grades/2205-duplex-stainless-steel) | ≥ 35 | SCC-resistant; chemical, offshore, brine |

## Quick Selection by Environment

- **Indoor dry / decorative** → 201 or 430 (430 for magnetic applications)
- **General industrial / food** → 304 / 304L (L variant for welded assemblies)
- **Coastal / chloride / marine** → 316 / 316L
- **Pharma / dairy / medical** → 316L with ASTM A270 Ra control
- **Chemical / brine / sour service** → 2205 Duplex (PREN ≥ 35)

Each grade page includes full chemistry to ASTM A240 / EN 10088-2 / GB / JIS, mechanical properties, PREN / CPT / CCT data, applicable standards, and recommended finishes.
