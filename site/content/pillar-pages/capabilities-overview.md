## Six Core Capabilities

- **[Surface Finishing](/solutions/capabilities/surface-finish-capability)** — No.8 Mirror (Ra < 0.05 µm, GU > 900 on a 24-unit grinding line) · AFP self-formulated 10–20 µm nano-coating · Super Finish with 6× corrosion resistance · plus No.4, Hairline, 2B, BA, Embossed, PVD color
- **[Cut-to-Length](/solutions/capabilities/cut-to-length)** — 15 KW fiber laser (≤ 50 mm plate), guillotine shearing, flatness ≤ 1 mm/m, length tolerance ±0.5 mm on precision orders
- **[Slitting & Edging](/solutions/capabilities/slitting-edging)** — ±0.1 mm width tolerance across the full thickness range; inline deburring
- **[Protective Coating](/solutions/capabilities/protective-coating)** — AFP nano-coating and PVD color applied in-house; formulation engineered per buyer reference sample
- **[Protective Film](/solutions/capabilities/protective-film)** — Novacel, Nitto, Polyfilm, PE, PVC — selected per downstream process (laser / AFP / fabrication)
- **[Packaging & Logistics](/solutions/capabilities/packaging-logistics)** — VCI wrap, wooden crate, ISPM 15 compliant, soft-cotton edge wrap for export; loading photos with every shipment
