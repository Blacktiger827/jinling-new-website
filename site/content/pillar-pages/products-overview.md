# Stainless Steel Products

*Coil, Sheet, Bar, and Tube — in 5 core grades, 8 surface finishes, all processed in-house in our 25,000 m² dual facility.*

Jinling Steel operates in Foshan, Guangdong since 1997. The full processing chain — slitting, cut-to-length, surface finishing, film lamination, and export packaging — happens under one roof, with Mill MTC traceability (type confirmed at order).

## Product Forms

- [Stainless Steel Coil](/products/stainless-steel-coil) — CR 0.3–3.0 mm, HR 3.0–14.0 mm
- [Stainless Steel Sheet & Plate](/products/stainless-steel-sheet) — 0.3–80 mm, cut-to-length
- [Stainless Steel Bar](/products/stainless-steel-bar) — Round, Flat, Angle, Square/Hex
- [Stainless Steel Tube & Pipe](/products/stainless-steel-tube-pipe) — Industrial, Sanitary, Decorative

## Signature Surface Finishes

- [No.8 Mirror Finish](/surfaces/stainless-steel-8k-mirror-finish) — Ra < 0.05 µm, 60° gloss > 900 GU
- [AFP Anti-Fingerprint](/surfaces/stainless-steel-afp-finish) — self-formulated 10–20 µm nano-coating
- **Super Finish** — 6× corrosion resistance vs. raw mill grade

- [304 / 304L](/grades/304-stainless-steel) — general-purpose, PREN ≈ 19
- [316 / 316L](/grades/316l-stainless-steel) — coastal, PREN ≈ 25
- [430](/grades/430-stainless-steel) — ferritic, No.8 Mirror capable
- [201](/grades/201-stainless-steel) — budget indoor decorative
- [2205 Duplex](/grades/2205-duplex-stainless-steel) — PREN ≥ 35, SCC-resistant
