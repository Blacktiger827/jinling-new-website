## Introduction: Why Single-Source Procurement Matters

Every supplier handoff in your stainless steel supply chain introduces measurable risk. When material moves from a mill to an independent slitter, then to a surface finisher, then to a separate packaging house, three things happen:

1. **Dimensional drift.** Each handler re-references the coil differently. Width tolerances that started at +0.0/鈭?.1 mm can compound to 卤0.3 mm by the time material reaches your factory.
2. **Surface damage.** Loading, unloading, and re-coiling between facilities account for the majority of scratches and dents on finished stainless steel 鈥?defects that only become visible after your fabricator removes the protective film.
3. **Documentation gaps.** When processing is outsourced, the original Mill Test Certificate (MTC 3.1) often does not follow the material through every step. You receive a certificate that covers chemistry and mechanical properties but cannot confirm which coil your cut sheets actually came from.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum order quantity?**
There is no fixed MOQ. Trial orders of 1鈥? tonnes per item are common for new clients. For processed material (custom slitting widths, specific cut lengths), a 2-tonne minimum per specification applies to maintain processing efficiency.

**Can you match a specific surface finish from a sample?**
Yes. Send us a 200 x 200 mm reference sample or a high-resolution photograph with Ra measurement if available. Our finishing team will produce a trial piece for your approval before production.

**How do you ensure traceability from mill to shipment?**
Every coil entering our facility is tagged with its MTC 3.1 reference. When we process that coil 鈥?slitting, cutting, finishing 鈥?the lot tracking system carries the original heat number through to the final packing list. You receive documentation linking your delivered sheets back to the specific mill heat.

**What payment terms do you offer?**
Standard terms: 30% deposit on order confirmation, 70% against copy of Bill of Lading. For established accounts with consistent order history, we offer 30鈥?0 day open account terms subject to credit review.

**Do you handle customs documentation for import?**
We provide commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading, MTC 3.1, Certificate of Origin, and fumigation certificate (for wooden crate packaging). For specific destination requirements (Form E, Form F, EUR.1), confirm at order stage and we will arrange accordingly.
