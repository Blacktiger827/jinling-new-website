# Stainless Steel Surface Finishes

*Three signature finishes engineered in-house — plus the full standard range.*

## Signature Finishes

- **[No.8 Mirror](/surfaces/stainless-steel-8k-mirror-finish)** — instrument-verified Ra < 0.05 µm and 60° gloss > 900 GU on a 24-unit automated grinding line. Capable on the 430 substrate, a Jinling differentiator most processors cannot match.
- **[AFP Anti-Fingerprint](/surfaces/stainless-steel-afp-finish)** — self-formulated 10–20 µm nano-coating, engineered per buyer reference sample for color, gloss, and roughness.
- **Super Finish** — proprietary polishing process delivering up to 6× corrosion resistance vs. raw ASTM/BS/JIS mill grade. Available on No.4 HL and SB profiles.

## Standard Range

- [No.4 Brushed](/surfaces/stainless-steel-no4-brushed-finish) — Ra 0.2–0.4 µm, unidirectional grain
- [Hairline](/surfaces/stainless-steel-hairline-finish) — continuous long-grain texture
- [2B Finish](/surfaces/stainless-steel-2b-finish) — skin-pass mill finish, matte-silver
- BA (Bright Annealed), Embossed (Stipple / Linen / Leather / Diamond), PVD Color — all processed in-house

Every finish is instrument-verified (Ra by profilometer, gloss by GU meter) and film-matched (Novacel, Nitto, Polyfilm) to the buyer's downstream process.
